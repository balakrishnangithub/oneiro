Oneiro is a free, open-source dream journal and lucid dreaming companion. Your dreams stay on your device; optional backup and multi-device sync is end-to-end encrypted to any WebDAV folder you control (pCloud, Nextcloud, …) or to a plain local folder.

Features:
• Dream journal — date-organized, searchable entries with lucid markers
• Reality-check reminders with a guided ritual (custom count and daytime window)
• Dream clues — gentle totem sounds during a night window to trigger lucidity
• Morning journal reminder and training pause
• Dream patterns — recurring themes and #hashtags across your journal
• Progress — streaks, lucid percentage, weekly activity, achievement milestones
• Voice dictation in the editor
• Optional PIN lock
• Encrypted sync ("OVault"): AES-256-GCM per entry, key derived from your passphrase with scrypt; zero-knowledge by design
• Import of Awoken plain-text journal exports; export to text or JSON
• Dark and light themes

No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no paywalls. Source code and full format documentation: see the project repository.
